Good to know

Why has my honey gone solid?

Raw honey crystallises over time. Stand the jar in warm water and it will turn liquid again.

How do you deliver?

Inside Dhaka in one to two days, and across Bangladesh by courier in three to five days. Cash on delivery is available.

Can I give it to my child?

Honey is not safe for babies under one year old. It is fine for older children.
