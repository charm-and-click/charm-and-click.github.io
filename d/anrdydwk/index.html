Questions

Can you work with us while we live abroad?

Yes. We meet on video calls, send weekly progress videos, and a family member can sign off on site if you like.

Do you only do full flats?

No. You can start with one room, such as the kitchen or the main bedroom, and add more later.

How long does a full flat take?

Most three-bedroom flats take 10 to 14 weeks from approved design to handover.

How do we pay?

In stages tied to the work: at design approval, at the start of building and at handover. Bank transfer or bKash.
