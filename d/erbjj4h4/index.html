Is everything halal?

Yes. All our meat is halal and we serve no alcohol. The drinks list is house sherbets, fresh juices and loose-leaf teas.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. Most guests dress for an evening out, but there is no rule.

Can the kitchen handle allergies?

Tell us in your booking message. The kitchen can adjust most dishes for nuts, gluten and dairy.

Do you take walk-ins?

When a table is free, yes. On Thursday and Friday nights, booking is the safer choice.

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes, for a small plating charge. Tell us when you book and we will bring it out with candles.
