Set 06

Questions

Is the free week really free?

Yes. Seven days with a coach, and no card or deposit. You only pay if you choose a membership on day seven.

Can I see the women's floor before I sign up?

Yes. A woman coach will show you around. Message us on WhatsApp and we will set a time.

I have never lifted. Where do I start?

With an empty bar and a coach beside you. Small group suits most beginners, because a coach checks every set.

How busy does it get?

Evenings from 5 to 9 PM are busiest. Mornings and midday are quiet. The planner above shows how busy your hour is.

Is there parking?

Yes. Members park in the basement, with a lift straight to both floors.
