Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Does scaling hurt?

Most people feel pressure, not pain. If your gums are sensitive, we numb the area first and work in short rounds with breaks.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Any plan above ৳30,000 can be spread over 6, 12 or 18 months, and the monthly amount is written into your plan.

How long do braces take?

Usually 12 to 20 months. You see us once a month for a short review, and every one of those visits is already in your price.

Is the estimate on this page the final price?

It is a close guide. At the free check-up your dentist looks at your teeth and X-ray, then gives you the final plan in writing.
